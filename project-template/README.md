# Project template: Community Knowledge Assistant

Each group names its assistant (ideally in Igbo) and builds it step by step, one step a day.

| Day | Step | Files you produce |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mon | Gather | `knowledge_base.csv` (columns: id, question_en, question_ig, answer_en, answer_ig, topic, source_url, source_name, added_by) |
| Tue | Understand | `router_report.csv`, `error_log.csv` |
| Wed | Extract | `glossary.csv` (term_en, term_ig, variants_ig, type, note) and `fact_cards.csv` (fact_id, kb_id, what, where, when, how_much, source_url) |
| Thu | Answer | `assistant_config.json`, `demo_log.csv` |
| Fri | Evaluate and present | `test_report.csv`, pitch slides or poster |

**Roles:** builder, language lead, sector lead, tester and ethics lead; the presenter rotates daily. Work in pairs, a builder with a non-coder, and let the non-coder drive.

**Saving:** at the end of every Lab B, save your files twice: to your group's Drive folder and to your USB stick.
